# Safety Invariants

1. No external publication, email, SMS, DM, quote send, tender submit, portal signup, fee payment, or terms acceptance without owner approval.
2. Public contact data is research evidence, not marketing consent.
3. Unknown consent, unknown token health, missing policy result, or closed kill switch means BLOCK.
4. Every mutation writes an audit/ledger event or returns failure.
5. Every external side-effect needs an idempotency key before execution.
6. Fugu Ultra is reserved for explicitly classified deep tasks; never for routine panel reads or polling.
7. Raw secrets, raw private PII, exact addresses, bank details, and raw customer media do not enter prompts, logs, tests, or owner-safe summaries.
8. B2B vendor/tender workflows remain draft/checklist/research until owner performs final portal action.
9. No scraping of Google Maps/GBP or protected portal data.
10. Tests must pass before service restart or enabling any new unit/timer.
