## 1. Quick summary

The synthesis is **internally coherent at the ID level** — every ID set (28 atoms, 18 primitives, 12 patterns, 13 anti-patterns, 16 invariants, 10 conflicts, 10 agent roles, 20 cross-map concepts) matches its declared count exactly, and there are **no dangling cross-references**. ## 2. Scope & method

- **Inputs audited:** all 18 uploaded files + the Master Handoff Prompt supplied mid-session.
- **Checks run:** (a) ID-set counts vs declared counts; (b) referential integrity of `AKO/PRIM/INV/PAT/AP/CFL` cross-references (referenced-but-undefined); (c) cross-file agreement on layer numbering, counts, open questions, and evidence status; (d) presence check of the "missing primaries" against actual bundle contents; (e) whether items marked `resolved`/`blocked` actually are.
- **Method note:** counts and referential integrity were verified programmatically (grep over the bundle), not by eye. Line-range citations *into* SRC-1 could **not** be verified because SRC-1 is not in the bundle (see F-5).

## 3. Findings

### 🔴 F-1 — The #1 "missing" primary is actually in the bundle (stale-status inversion)

**Claim under audit:** `MissingEvidenceRegister.md` MER-1 marks DOC-B (`OCTOPUS_CHRONO_ARCHITECTURE.md`) as PARTIAL and lists as **BLOCKED without it**: "complete/verbatim TINV-1..7 definitions, the LANGAR SQL schema, pacemaker + phi-accrual pseudocode, exact `age_tick` advancement rule." `DocumentMap.yml` lists it under `missing_primary`; `index.yml` under `missing_primaries`; `ProjectState.md` §3/§7, `FinalAnswer.md`, `Roadmap.md`, `UnifiedArchitecture.md` (L0 "🟡 MER-1"), `Graphs.yml` (L0 `blocked_by: MER-1`), and `MasterSystemPrompt.md` ("pending original substrate docs") all repeat this.

**Reality:** `OCTOPUS CHRONO ARCHITECTURE.md` **is in the bundle** and contains every allegedly-blocked internal:
- §7 — full **TINV-1…TINV-7** definitions (verbatim, not just names).
- §8 — the **`langar_ledger` SQL DDL** plus `heartbeat`, `leg_clock`, `experience_meter`, `duration_marker`, `anticipation_queue`.
- §9 — **pacemaker + phi-accrual pseudocode** (`heartbeat_loop`, `leg_loop`, `on_human_judgment`).
- §7/§8/§11 — the **`age_tick` rule** (`is_human=1` only).

**Impact:** The following flip from PARTIAL/BLOCKED → **READY** the moment the register is refreshed: `02_AtomicKnowledge` (DOC-B internals: AKO-011 LANGAR schema, AKO-013 TINV-7), `05_Graphs/DependencyGraph` (L0 `blocked_by` clears), `10_Implementation/DataSchemas` + `/APIs`, and **OQ-2** (see F-2). This is the project's own declared "upgrade trigger" for MER-1 — it has fired.

**Fix:** Run the MER-1 upgrade: re-extract DOC-B internals verbatim, emit a `10_Implementation/DataSchemas.sql` from §8, clear the `blocked_by`/`🟡 MER-1` flags in `Graphs.yml` L0 and `UnifiedArchitecture.md` L0, and move DOC-B out of `missing_primary` in `DocumentMap.yml` / `index.yml`.

**Fix:** Renumber `Graphs.yml`'s `dependency_graph` to the canonical L0–L13 (+Lp) scheme: Guards→L8, Observability→L13, EvolutionDoctor→L7, Business→L9, Research→L10; update `critical_path` to `L13-checkpoint … → L7`.

### 🟡 F-5 — The Level-A backbone (SRC-1) is absent; half the corpus's A-claims are unverifiable from this bundle

**Present primaries in bundle:** SRC-2 (`OCTOPUS HYBRID SYNTHESIS.md`) and DOC-B (`OCTOPUS CHRONO ARCHITECTURE.md`).
**Absent:** **SRC-1** (the 2688-line file that `DocumentMap.yml` says holds **DOC-01…DOC-07**), DOC-A ("Survival Stack"), and file-3 (lab-seed JSON).

**Consequence:** Every Level-A citation of the form `SRC-1:xxx-yyy` — i.e. the entire **Ari OS half** (DOC-02: Event Bus, 7 Guards, Personal-State, Modes, Worker isolation), plus **research/business/epistemic** halves (DOC-01/03/04/05/07) — cannot be verified against a primary in this bundle. `README.md` and `DocumentMap.yml` assert "SRC-1 in context"; that was true during synthesis but is **false for this handoff bundle**. Since the Master Handoff's stated purpose is to onboard an agent "with zero prior context," a cold reader literally cannot reach the operator/business/research primaries.

**Note on the inversion:** the register's bookkeeping is backwards — the doc it calls *missing* (DOC-B) is here; the doc it treats as *in-context* (SRC-1) is not.

**Fix:** Add SRC-1 (and ideally DOC-A, file-3) to the canonical bundle; **or** re-tag the DOC-01…07 atoms as "A — source not in handoff bundle" so their status is honest for cold onboarding.

### 🟡 F-6 — The Master Handoff advances CFL-03 (the "biggest residual risk") but the repo doesn't reflect it

The **Master Handoff §5.1–5.2** supplies a concrete, if `[EST]`, mitigation the synthesis lacks: per-worker non-root process, `seccomp-bpf`, dropped caps + user namespace, `cgroups v2` (also enforces cost-cap/anti-spawn-bomb), read-only rootfs, brokered egress, and a **D1–D5 defense-in-depth** model whose key lever (D3: structural output confinement → "injection buys one item in a human-reviewed queue") is architecturally sound and consistent with INV-01/03/11.

**Fix:** Fold Handoff §5 into `08_Safety` / L8 as an `[EST]` mitigation for CFL-03, **preserving** the honest residual-risk caveat (model-layer injection + shared-silicon side-channels remain, caught by human review + Critic). This upgrades CFL-03 from "open" to "partially mitigated (design-level, unproven)."

## 6. Gap-report (what this audit did not / could not verify)

- **SRC-1, DOC-A, file-3 not in bundle** → all `SRC-1:*` line citations and the DOC-01/02/03/04/05/07 A-claims are taken on trust here, not verified (F-5). This audit verifies *internal* consistency and *DOC-B/SRC-2* content directly; it cannot confirm the Ari-OS half against a primary.
- **No runtime/behavioral verification** — this is a document audit. TINV enforceability, the metabolic `rate↔age` coupling, and phi-accrual tuning are code-and-test questions, not doc questions.
- **Confidence scores (0–100) were not re-derived** — I checked that they are present and internally plausible, not that each number is calibrated.
- **Persian-language primaries (SRC-2, DOC-B)** were read in full; if a nuance depends on exact Persian phrasing in a spec line, a native re-read is advisable before hard-committing schema names.
