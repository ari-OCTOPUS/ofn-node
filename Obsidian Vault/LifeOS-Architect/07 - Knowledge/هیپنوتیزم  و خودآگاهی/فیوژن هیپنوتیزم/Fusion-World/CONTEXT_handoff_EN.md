Key learnings & principles

Conflating the two investigative cases (real physics anomaly detection vs. canon pattern-finding) leads to self-deception and must be actively guarded against
Three anti-self-deception tools apply to this project: pre-registration, the look-elsewhere effect / multiple comparisons problem (with Bonferroni correction), and negative controls with "iron-clad alibis"
The no-communication theorem is a hard constraint that cannot be resolved within established physics, even if canon entanglement mechanics are confirmed
