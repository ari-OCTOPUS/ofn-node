# Lead (painting, Sydney) — Research Digest 2026-07-06

## Today's question
What is the average **job value** for Sydney painting work in 2025–2026 by segment — interior residential, full exterior, strata/body-corporate, and builder/new-build — so we can apply yesterday's **20%-of-job-value rule** and rank segments for Experiment #1 (SEGMENT-DISCOVERY)? (direct follow-up to 2026-07-05 spore #1)

## Findings

- **Interior residential (Sydney 2026): single room $345–$920 (typical ~$575); full 3-bed interior $4,500–$8,500 (typical ~$5,750–$7,500); 4-bed $6,000–$14,000. Rates $18–$45/sqm interior, $45–$115/hr (typical ~$80).** Multiple independent 2026 Sydney guides converge tightly on these bands ([What's The Damage](https://whatsthedamage.com.au/painter-cost-sydney/), [Brushworks](https://brushworks.com.au/house-painting-cost/), [MK Master](https://www.mkmaster.au/blog/house-painting-cost-sydney-guide), [Quote Yard NSW](https://www.thequoteyard.com.au/services/house-painting-cost-2026-nsw)).
  - *For lead-gen:* at ~$250 marketplace cost-per-won-job, a full 3-bed interior absorbs it at ~3–4% of job value — fine. A single-room/touch-up job ($345–$920) bleeds 27–70%+ — **kill the low-ticket segment on paid channels immediately**; it's only viable via free/owned channels (GBP, referral, Airtasker review-building).

- **Full exterior (Sydney 2026): single-storey 3-bed $5,000–$12,000; two-storey $8,000–$18,000+ (scaffolding alone $4,000–$8,000); $20–$60/sqm.** Exterior consistently prices 20–40% above interior due to prep, weather and access ([Quote Yard](https://www.thequoteyard.com.au/services/house-painting-cost-2026-nsw), [MK Master](https://www.mkmaster.au/blog/house-painting-cost-sydney-guide)).
  - *For lead-gen:* exterior repaints are the sweet spot for *paid* residential lead spend — $250 CPWJ is 1.4–3% of job value, and the scaffolding line-item means competitors' quotes vary wildly, which rewards our structured-quote/fast-response play.

- **Strata/body-corporate is an order of magnitude bigger per job: small complex (6–10 lots) $8,000–$25,000; medium (20–50 lots) $25,000–$80,000; large (50+) $80,000–$300,000+. Common-area interiors $15–$35/sqm, facades $20–$60/sqm; rope access +20–40%, after-hours +15–30%.** Governed by the Strata Schemes Management Act 2015 — owners-corporation approval thresholds, capital-works-fund budgeting and insurance requirements shape procurement ([Quote Yard strata guide](https://www.thequoteyard.com.au/services/strata-painting-cost-2026-nsw)).
  - *For lead-gen:* strata is where job value lives, but it's **not a marketplace channel** — leads come via strata managers, tenders and maintenance-cycle relationships, with longer sales cycles and compliance overhead. For a solo operator it likely means subcontracting to or partnering with an established strata painter first. Experiment #1 should treat strata as a separate *channel* hypothesis, not just a segment.

- **Margins invert the raw job-value ranking: NSW painting contractors net 12–22%; high-volume operators (typical of builder/new-build and big tender work) run leanest at ~8–12%, while niche/specialist work can hit 30%.** Sydney is flagged as the most competitive, price-pressured market in the country ([Painters Link margin guide](https://painterslink.com.au/average-profit-margin-painting-contractor/)).
  - *For lead-gen:* builder/new-build subcontract work = volume at thin margin with the builder owning the customer — it's cash-flow filler, not a lead-gen target. Ranking for Experiment #1 by (job value × margin × channel-cost fit): **1) exterior residential repaint, 2) whole-interior repaint, 3) strata (via relationship channel), 4) builder subbie (only as gap-filler)**. Note: hard $/sqm data for builder subcontract rates is thin in public sources — one source even claims repaints price 10–20% *below* new builds, contradicting its own claim elsewhere; treat new-build economics as `[To verify]`.

- **Labour benchmarks for our own quoting engine: Sydney hourly $45–$115 (most ~$70–$80), day rate $400–$640 (premium crews to $800/day), labour ≈70% of job cost, materials ≈20–30%.** ([Quote Yard pricing guide](https://www.thequoteyard.com.au/services/painter-pricing-guide-2026-nsw), [Painters Link day rates](https://painterslink.com.au/how-much-do-painters-charge-per-day-in-australia/)).
  - *For lead-gen:* these are the sanity-check constants for the bot's auto-quote drafts — a quote implying <$45/hr effective labour is underpriced (and looks unlicensed); >$115/hr needs premium justification in the quote copy.
