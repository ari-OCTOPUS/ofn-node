# Jobs/Trade-Market (painting, Sydney) — Research Digest 2026-07-06

## Today's question
What is the realistic average **total job value** for residential, strata, and exterior painting jobs in Sydney 2026 — the denominator the `lead` scout's 20%-rule/CPWJ model needs (2026-07-05 spore #1) — and are current painter labour rates/marketplace structure shifting under it?

## Findings

- **Interior/exterior per-m² rates broadly confirm the internal OPS-01 estimate, with exterior full-job totals diverging by source type.** Sydney interior wall painting sits at $20–35/m² (2 coats, standard prep) and standard exterior at $20–35/m². A full 3-bed interior repaint is consistently quoted $4,500–8,000 across sources — matches OPS-01 exactly. But **full 3-bed exterior repaint** varies sharply: SEO "pricing guide" sites (thequoteyard.com.au, brushworks.com.au — templated content-farm pattern, near-identical text across every service/suburb page) say $3,500–7,500, while **Oneflare's own cost guide** (an actual marketplace with real transaction data, not a lead-gen content farm) says $8,000–18,000 for full exterior. [source](https://www.thequoteyard.com.au/services/3-bedroom-house-painting-cost-2026-nsw) · [source](https://www.oneflare.com.au/costs/painters)
  - *For lead-gen:* treat the SEO-network figures as lower-confidence (single-source-of-truth risk — many domains, one template) and weight Oneflare's higher exterior band more; use **$8k–18k** not $5k–10k for exterior-job CPWJ modelling until the bot logs real quotes. This changes the 20%-rule math favourably — exterior jobs absorb marketplace CPL even more easily than assumed.

- **Combined interior+exterior full repaint: $9,000–22,000+.** This is the single highest-value residential segment and should be the primary upsell target once a lead is engaged (interior-only or exterior-only leads should always be quoted for the combo). [source](https://www.thequoteyard.com.au/services/painting-cost-per-square-metre-2026-nsw)
  - *For lead-gen:* strengthens yesterday's "high-ticket residential absorbs marketplace CPL" finding — a combo quote nearly doubles job value on the same lead-acquisition cost, which could be the single biggest lever on the 20%-rule gate.

- **Strata contract value ranges enormously by building size and is the highest-ceiling segment: ~$3,000/unit (small townhouse complex) up to $10k–20k (small strata), $50k+ (multi-storey), and $200k+ (large/high-rise).** [source](https://painterslink.com.au/the-cost-to-paint-a-strata-building/)
  - *For lead-gen:* for Experiment #1 (segment discovery), strata is a fat-tail bet — low win-frequency (committee decisions, longer sales cycle, formal quoting per OPS-01 §5) but a single win can equal 10–50 residential jobs. Segment ranking should score strata by **expected value** (win-rate × contract size), not raw win-rate; don't discount it just because it converts less often than residential leads.

- **Painter labour rates: $45–110/hr depending on experience/source (qualified residential painters cluster $50–90/hr), day rate $400–650/day; Sydney runs 15–25% above regional NSW, inner/eastern suburbs 15–25% above outer-metro.** Fair Work's 2026 Annual Wage Review lifted award minimums 4.75% and the national minimum wage 6% to $26.44/hr, effective first full pay period after 1 July 2026 — relevant if/when subcontracting labour rather than doing solo owner-operator work. [source](https://www.thequoteyard.com.au/services/painter-hourly-rate-2026-nsw) · [source](https://www.fwc.gov.au/registered-organisations/find-registered-organisation/master-painters-australia-nsw-association-inc)
  - *For lead-gen:* confirms OPS-01's rate logic is not stale; no update needed to the day-rate assumptions embedded in the quoting framework.

- **[New, verified — market-structure shift] Oneflare (Airtasker-owned since 2022) permanently closed 30 June 2026 — six days before this digest.** Existing Oneflare tradie/customer accounts do not carry over; users are being funnelled to Airtasker. This directly changes the marketplace landscape the `lead` scout mapped on 2026-07-04/05 (hipages vs Oneflare comparisons). [source](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/oneflare/__YxDt0cDA9zENkJepr3AXDm-d4tyWB1BoRrBm1LsX8qc)
  - *For lead-gen:* one fewer independent shared-lead marketplace in Sydney painting = less price competition among platforms (hipages' pricing power likely firms up) but also a fresh pool of ex-Oneflare tradies migrating to Airtasker/hipages, raising quote-pool crowding right when we'd be onboarding. Any earlier plan or note that names Oneflare as an active channel option needs a correction flag.
