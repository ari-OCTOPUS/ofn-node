# Mining — Research Digest 2026-07-06

## Today's question
Yesterday's spore #1: does VerusHash 2.2 merge-mining (VRSC + live PBaaS chains) from one Orange Pi 5 Pro's ~6.6 MH/s turn the sub-cent solo payout into something non-trivial — which PBaaS chains are actually live and what is the *combined* payout?

## Findings

- **Answer to the spore: NO — merge-mining does not rescue the economics; it adds roughly +3–10% on top of a sub-cent day.** Computed from live network stats and per-block emissions: at ~6.6 MH/s the rig earns ~0.0158 VRSC/day (VRSC net ~900 GH/s, 3/block, ~720 miner blocks/day) ≈ $0.007–0.012. The three merged chains add: CHIPS ~0.0049/day (~$0.0002 at ~$0.04), vARRR ~0.0015/day (cap $1.9M, price well under $1 → ≲$0.001), vDEX ~0.000055/day (~$0.00003 at ~$0.5). PBaaS per-block emissions are tiny (vARRR 0.08, CHIPS 0.0397, vDEX 0.003885 vs VRSC's 3.0). **What this means for the rig/strategy:** yesterday's "first rig-favoring angle" survives only as a *free option*, not a game-changer — take the bonus (zero marginal watt), but stop expecting merge-mining to make one board an income unit. [source](https://verus.farm/stats)

- **Only 3 of the "up to 22" merge-mineable chains are actually live on pools: vARRR, vDEX, CHIPS.** The 22-chain figure on verus.io is protocol capacity, not current reality. Pools with merge-mining support: Verus Farm (0.3% fee), LuckPool (1%), An Interesting Hole (1%), Paddy Pool (3%), CiscoTech (1%), official Verus Pool (5%, donated); ZergPool/Vipor/Wattpool do NOT merge-mine. **What this means:** if the board mines at all, point it at a merge-mining pool (Verus Farm's 0.3% is the cheapest) — same shares, three extra coin streams. Solo merge-mining would need a full daemon per chain (multi-GB RAM each), impractical next to pool mining on an SBC. [source](https://docs.verus.io/economy/start-mining.html)

- **PBaaS network hashrates are 25–37% of VRSC's (CHIPS ~231 GH/s, vARRR ~260 GH/s, vDEX ~338 GH/s vs VRSC ~900 GH/s), so the rig's *share* on merged chains is ~3–4× larger — but per-block emissions are so small it doesn't matter.** The higher share is fully offset by micro-emissions and micro-prices. **What this means:** don't be lured by "lower difficulty" on PBaaS chains — the Coin Scouting Framework's screen must always multiply share × emission × price, never stop at difficulty. [source](https://verus.aninterestinghole.xyz/stats)

- **Liquidity for PBaaS coins lives almost entirely on Verus's own protocol DEX (basket currencies), not exchanges: vDEX ~$90k and CHIPS ~$65k total in on-chain baskets; caps vARRR $1.9M / CHIPS $0.8M / vDEX $0.43M.** Exit path for mined PBaaS coins is on-chain conversion → VRSC → then off. **What this means:** merged-coin income is triple-illiquid (micro-cap, DEX-only, VRSC itself thin) — book it as speculative dust, and the death-watch screen should track basket reserves shrinking as an early death signal for these chains. [source](https://cryptodashboard.faldt.net/)

- **Pricing anomaly worth flagging: VRSC trades ~$0.64–0.76 on the Verus DEX baskets vs ~$0.44 on CEXs (CoinGecko feed) — a ~50–70% gap.** Same dashboard shows the DEX-side BTC/ETH prices roughly at parity, so this is VRSC-specific: CEX volume is so thin that the on-chain DEX is arguably the truer price. **What this means:** payout estimates swing ~1.7× depending on which price you believe ($0.007 vs $0.012/day); any future "mine-to-hold VRSC" thesis should value the stack at the price it can actually *exit* at — and the gap itself is a signal the crypto scout should examine. [source](https://cryptodashboard.faldt.net/)

- **ARM/SBC merge-mining is now mainstream practice: a maintained RK3588/Orange Pi 5 setup guide (Oink70 ccminer ARM64 build) plus March 2026 community reports of phones mining VRSC+vARRR+vDEX+CHIPS simultaneously via pools.** No special config needed — the pool handles the merge; the miner just submits VerusHash shares. **What this means:** the bench experiment (SCOUT-B) is trivial to set up: Oink70 ccminer → Verus Farm → measure combined 4-coin payout-per-watt in one run. [source](https://github.com/bokiko/Verus-ARM64-Mining)
