# Accounting (AU) — Research Digest 2026-07-05

## Today's question
For Ari's **Pty Ltd** (the D3 structure), what are the **new ASIC fees and annual-review obligations that took effect on 1 July 2026** (FY2026–27 indexation), and what changed from FY2025–26? — a direct pickup of yesterday's spore about the "live 1 July 2026 indexed schedule", but re-aimed from sole-trader/business-name to the **company** obligations that actually sit in this project's compliance register.

## Findings

- **The annual review fee for a proprietary company rose to $342 from 1 July 2026 (up from $329 — +$13).** ASIC bills this every year on the company's **annual review date** (the anniversary of registration) and it must be paid **within 2 months** of that date. *What this means for bookkeeping:* this is the fixed, recurring ASIC cost of keeping the Pty Ltd alive — budget **$342/yr** as a GST-free expense, and fill the currently-blank "ASIC annual review date" row in the compliance register from the ACN registration anniversary so the invoice is never a surprise. *(General info from the official indexed schedule — the exact amount and date for this specific company appear on ASIC's own annual statement; confirm with the accountant.)* [source](https://www.asic.gov.au/for-business-and-companies/forms-and-fees/all-fees/fee-indexation/)

- **Late-payment penalties on the annual review fee also increased on 1 July 2026 to $102 (up to 1 month late) and $428 (more than 1 month late)** — up from $98 / $411. These stack on top of the $342 fee. *What this means for bookkeeping:* missing the 2-month window is disproportionately expensive — a $342 obligation becomes **$444** if <1 month late and **$770** if >1 month late. The mitigation is purely operational: a recurring reminder ~2 weeks before the review date so the fee is set aside, not scrambled for. *(General info — ASIC can waive a late fee for circumstances beyond your control, but that's discretionary, not a plan.)* [source](https://www.asic.gov.au/for-business-and-companies/companies/company-annual-review/late-company-annual-review-fee/)

- **If the Pty Ltd is not yet registered (the register still shows ACN "to measure"), the cost of registering has risen to $636 from 1 July 2026 (up from $611), with name reservation now $65 and voluntary deregistration $52.** *What this means for tax/setup:* this is a one-off capital/establishment cost feeding directly into the still-open D3 "registered or in progress" decision — the entry price for a company went up $25 on 1 July, so any pending incorporation is now $636 through ASIC (before any agent/accountant fee). *(General info — standard proprietary-company-with-share-capital fee; a registered agent or lawyer may bundle this with a service fee.)* [source](https://growsmsf.com.au/asic-fees-2026/)

- **If the business trades under a name other than the exact company name, the ASIC business-name renewal also reindexed on 1 July 2026 to ~$47 (1 year) and ~$108 (3 years)** — up from $44 / $102 — and is a **separate** registration from both the ABN (free, via the ABR) and the company itself. *What this means for bookkeeping:* a trading/brand name (e.g. a "Ziman"- or painting-branded name) is its own line on the annual compliance calendar; the 3-year option is cheaper per year and removes two renewal events from the reminder list. *(General info — the live business-name fee is on ASIC's business-names schedule and updates every 1 July.)* [source](https://www.asic.gov.au/for-business-and-companies/forms-and-fees/all-fees/fees-for-commonly-lodged-documents/)

- **The mechanism, not just the numbers: ASIC fees are indexed every 1 July to the previous March-quarter CPI (Corporations (Review Fees) Act 2003 / Business Names Registration (Fees) Regulations 2022) and are GST-free.** *What this means for bookkeeping:* expect a small rise **every** 1 July, so any cached figure in a note goes stale annually — and because ASIC fees carry **no GST, no GST credit is claimable** on them (code them GST-free, not GST-inclusive). A **10-year prepayment** of the annual review fee is available at a fixed rate as a hedge against future indexation for a company you intend to keep. *(General info — indexation is legislated; the discounted 10-year rate is on ASIC's "pay 10 years in advance" page.)* [source](https://www.asic.gov.au/for-business-and-companies/forms-and-fees/all-fees/fee-indexation/)
