# Crypto — Research Digest 2026-07-04 (live kickoff)

## Today's question
For a tiny (~AU$30/month) eToro-style account, which sentiment + on-chain signals are actually decision-grade, what exit/risk rules turn them into trades, and can the whole data stack be assembled for the budget?

## Findings
- **LunarCrush's decision-grade metrics are Galaxy Score (0–100 blended health of price + social impact + sentiment + price/social correlation) and AltRank (a coin's rank vs 4,000+ assets on combined social + price + volume momentum) — and API access now starts at ~US$72/mo (Individual), well above budget, while the free "Hobby" tier gives market data but *no social data and no API*.** For our budget this means treating the LunarCrush *web dashboard's* Galaxy Score / AltRank as a manual weekly read rather than an automated feed — the raw social signal has to be sampled by eye, not piped. Best signal: a coin climbing AltRank toward #1 = strongest combined social + financial momentum. [source](https://lunarcrush.com/about/api)
- **On-chain composite indicators turned broadly bearish in early Dec 2025 — CryptoQuant CEO Ki Young Ju publicly flagged "most Bitcoin on-chain indicators are bearish," a verified-cycle-top style warning tied to their composite dashboards and global-liquidity framework.** For the portfolio this is a macro caution flag: the reliable *accumulation* setup is the inverse (low MVRV + SOPR below 1.0 + sustained exchange *outflows*); a top-warning is high MVRV + declining SOPR + rising exchange *inflows*. Maps directly onto our "Washout signal" template (capitulation volume + on-chain exchange exits). [source](https://cryptoslate.com/bitcoin-on-chain-data-just-flashed-critical-bearish-signal-that-cryptoquant-warns-marks-a-verified-cycle-top/)
- **The core exit-rule design consensus for 2025–26: risk ≤1% of the account per trade, demand ≥1:2 reward:risk, and use *tiered* (staged) stop-losses/take-profits rather than a single line — e.g. take profit at +30% / +60% / +100% and a "moon-bag" rule of withdrawing principal once a position doubles while keeping 10–20% to run.** For a ~AU$30-scale account this is the crucial discipline: single stops get wicked out by crypto volatility even when the thesis is intact, so pre-placed laddered TPs plus a calendar review date (12–15 months) convert signals into a mechanical, emotion-free exit — and pair cleanly with the project's Fractional-Kelly sizing (¼–½ Kelly). [source](https://coinbureau.com/guides/crypto-exit-strategies)
- **A genuinely usable data stack fits the budget: CoinGecko's free "Demo" tier gives 10,000 calls/month at 100 calls/min (12+ years of historical price/mcap/volume, on-chain DEX coverage across 250 networks, no credit card), and the first paid tier ("Basic") is only US$35/mo (~AU$54) for 100,000 calls/min bump — so market + on-chain-DEX data can be free, leaving the tiny budget for one paid add-on.** This is the backbone of a legitimate under-budget quant loop: use CoinGecko free for price/market/DEX data and spend nothing there. [source](https://www.coingecko.com/en/api/pricing)
- **Tested 2026 dashboard builds show the sweet-spot low-cost stack is ~US$0–20/month: CoinGecko Portfolio (free) + TradingView Free/Pro ($0–14.95) + Glassnode free tier for limited on-chain + LunarCrush free-tier sentiment — with the tested finding that assets with positive weighted social sentiment outperformed negative-sentiment ones by ~23% over 90-day windows, and Glassnode's whale/accumulation alerts led major moves by 6–48h in 83% of cases.** For us this is the concrete blueprint: monitor weekly RSI on top holdings, exchange-flow alerts, weighted sentiment, and MVRV Z-Score — all obtainable at/near free tier — and treat sentiment as a *confirming* edge, not a standalone entry. [source](https://theledgermind.com/crypto-portfolio-dashboard-setup/)
- **A full real-time crypto analytics pipeline can run for ~US$0.01/month by streaming Binance's no-auth WebSocket into local Docker (Redpanda/Flink/Airflow), landing data in Google Cloud Storage, and using BigQuery *Load Jobs* (free) instead of Streaming Inserts (~$0.01/200MB) — enriched daily with CoinGecko's free Demo API and visualised on Grafana Cloud's free tier.** The load-bearing trick for our AU$30 ceiling: never use paid streaming inserts; accept ~1-minute data staleness (invisible on a 30-sec-refresh dashboard) and every external service in the pipeline has a real free tier. This shows the *automation* half of the stack (not just manual dashboards) is achievable essentially for free. [source](https://dev.to/derrickryangiggs/i-built-a-real-time-crypto-analytics-pipeline-for-001month-heres-the-full-architecture-pfl)
