# Lead (painting, Sydney) — Research Digest 2026-07-05

## Today's question
For a new/small Sydney painting business, what is the realistic **lead-to-job win rate** on pay-per-lead marketplaces (hipages/Oneflare) — how many tradies is each lead sold to, and what does that imply for the **true cost-per-won-job** vs the ~$30–$80 sticker CPL, compared to owned channels (Google Business Profile / SEO)? (builds on 2026-07-04 spore #1 + Open Question #3)

## Findings

- **Each hipages lead is sold to "up to 3" tradies — confirmed by hipages' own FY25 results, not just blogs.** hipages' investor deck defines a paid connection as "up to 3 tradies can successfully claim a lead," and every job is connected with up to 3. But in oversaturated Sydney metro categories (painting included), you can be "one of ~58 tradies *responding*" to a job even if only 3 *connect* — so response-speed pressure is brutal. [source](https://hotcopper.com.au/data/announcements/ASX/2A1615414_HPG.pdf) · [source](https://tradiescaler.com/lead-generation/hipages-vs-oneflare/)
  - *For lead-gen:* the "shared 3 ways" figure is real and vendor-confirmed — model win-rate assuming you're always quoting against 2+ others, and treat sub-5-minute response as non-negotiable, not optional.

- **Platform-average close rate on shared leads is ~1-in-5 (20%); the realistic band is 15–25%, with strong operators hitting 1-in-3 (33%) and weak ones 1-in-8 (~12%).** Multiple independent AU sources converge here (M4T, ServiceScale, 20MinuteMarketing, tradiescaler's "25–40% for active responsive tradies"). The number that matters is jobs won ÷ *quotes sent*, not ÷ leads received. [source](https://www.20minutemarketing.com.au/blog/is-hipages-worth-it) · [source](https://made4tradies.com.au/blog/is-hipages-worth-it-for-tradies)
  - *For lead-gen:* our pipeline must log two funnel stages separately (lead→quoted, quoted→won). Assume **20% baseline** for cost modelling until the bot's real data says otherwise; don't plan on the 33% "strong operator" number in year one.

- **True cost-per-won-job on marketplaces is ~$150–$375+ (before subscription), i.e. 3–5× the sticker CPL.** At a painter-typical $25–$35/lead (painters/cleaners sit at the low end) and 20% close, lead spend alone is ~$125–$175 per won job; add the $200–$600/mo subscription and it lands $150–$375+. ServiceScale models it as a "~21% marketing tax" on job value. [source](https://www.servicescale.com.au/blog/why-hipages-costs-87-per-booked-job-not-the-19-lead-fee-they-advertise) · [source](https://dossisdigital.com.au/is-hipages-worth-it-for-tradies/)
  - *For lead-gen:* the **20%-of-job-value rule** is the decision gate — a $3–5k residential/exterior repaint absorbs a $250 CPWJ easily (~5–8%), but a $300–500 touch-up job bleeds money. This is direct ammunition for **Experiment #1 (segment discovery)**: high-ticket residential/strata repaints are where marketplace CPL is survivable; low-ticket work is not.

- **Owned channels (Google Business Profile + local SEO) convert 2–3× better and cost ~$0/lead after setup.** GBP/organic leads are "effectively free per lead after setup" (Golden Marketing's Sydney 2026 benchmark), close at 20–40% (searcher already picked you), and mature owned-channel cost-per-booked-job settles at $20–$50 vs marketplaces' $150–$375. Direct/phone leads convert 38–52% vs hipages 14–22%. [source](https://goldenmarketing.com.au/blog/cost-per-lead-sydney-tradies)
  - *For lead-gen:* confirms sequencing — GBP first (owned, compounding, cheapest CPWJ), marketplaces only as gap-filler for high-ticket segments. Our AI layer's highest ROI is squeezing the *conversion* lever (5-min response, structured quoting, review velocity) which halves CPWJ on the *same* spend.

- **[Verified — overturns a competing claim] Google Local Services Ads are still NOT available in Australia (2026).** One AU marketing blog claimed "LSAs now operate in major Australian cities at $15–$50/verified lead" — this is false. Google's own sign-up flow lists only 11 eligible countries (US, Canada, UK, Ireland, Austria, Belgium, France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Switzerland); there is no AU trade category and no "select cities" workaround. [source](https://searchscope.com.au/local-seo/google-local-service-ads-for-businesses/)
  - *For lead-gen:* re-confirms yesterday's finding — any plan assuming exclusive LSA pay-per-lead for Sydney is dead. The AU pay-per-lead reality remains shared marketplaces (hipages/Oneflare/ServiceSeeking) + geo-targeted Google/Meta ads.
