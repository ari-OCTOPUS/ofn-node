# Accounting (AU) — Research Digest 2026-07-06

## Today's question
**Payday Super went live 5 days ago (1 July 2026).** The compliance register only has a one-line mention ("از 2026-07-01 پرداخت super هر payday"). What are the actual final rules — and what must the Pty Ltd have in place *before* the painting business makes its first hire? (New signal: previous digests covered ATO deadlines/thresholds on 07-04 and ASIC fees on 07-05; Payday Super mechanics were never researched.)

## Findings

- **Payday Super is fully legislated and live — this is law, not proposal.** The Treasury Laws Amendment (Payday Superannuation) Bill 2025 and Superannuation Guarantee Charge Amendment Bill 2025 received Royal Assent on 6 November 2025 and took effect 1 July 2026. Employers must now pay super guarantee **every payday** (not quarterly), and contributions must be **received and allocatable by the employee's super fund within 7 business days after payday**. Exception: **20 business days** for a new employee's first contribution or a first payment to a new fund. *What this means for bookkeeping:* super becomes a per-pay-cycle cash-flow item, not a quarterly one — the moment the painting business hires, super must be budgeted into every single pay run. *(General info — legislated; specific application to confirm with the accountant.)* [ATO — About Payday Super](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/super-for-employers/payday-super/about-payday-super) · [Parliament — Bills Digest](https://www.aph.gov.au/Parliamentary_Business/Bills_Legislation/bd/bd2526/26bd025)

- **Super is now 12% of "qualifying earnings" (QE), not OTE, and QE must be reported in STP every payday.** QE bundles ordinary time earnings, all commissions, salary-sacrifice contributions and other amounts previously counted for SG. From 1 July 2026 employers report **both QE and super liability** through Single Touch Payroll each payday (new "Label Q"), giving the ATO near real-time visibility of super owed vs paid. *What this means for bookkeeping:* payroll software must be QE-capable and pay codes correctly mapped **before** the first pay run — a miscoded allowance now produces an automatic, visible shortfall rather than something fixable at quarter-end. *(General info.)* [ATO — qualifying earnings](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/super-for-employers/paying-super-on-payday/what-payments-are-qualifying-earnings) · [ATO — managing the changeover](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/super-for-employers/payday-super/payday-super-how-to-manage-super-during-the-changeover)

- **The ATO's free Small Business Superannuation Clearing House (SBSCH) is gone as of 1 July 2026** — closed to new users since 1 Oct 2025, shut for everyone since 30 June 2026, no grace period. Replacements: payroll software with built-in super payments (Xero, MYOB — incl. MYOB Solo, Reckon), a commercial clearing house, or a large super fund's employer portal; must be SuperStream-compliant. Funds now have only **3 business days** (was 20) to allocate or bounce a contribution, and the New Payments Platform enables near-instant transfers. *What this means for bookkeeping:* the "free ATO option" for a micro-employer no longer exists — the choice of accounting/payroll software (an open decision in PROJECT.md) now doubles as the super-payment channel decision, so pick software with integrated super payments rather than bolting on a separate clearing house. *(General info — pricing of alternatives changes; verify current plans.)* [ATO — SBSCH](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/super-for-employers/paying-super-contributions/how-to-pay-super/small-business-superannuation-clearing-house) · [ATO — SBSCH is closing](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/small-business-newsroom/the-small-business-superannuation-clearing-house-is-closing)

- **The redesigned super guarantee charge (SGC) is harsher on timing but softer on tax.** Old regime: self-assessed via SG statement, 10% flat interest, flat admin fee, **not** deductible, penalties up to 200%. New regime from 1 July 2026: **ATO-assessed automatically** (no SG statement — the ATO sees the shortfall through STP), calculated on QE, **daily-compounding interest at the GIC rate**, an "administrative uplift" amount (reducible via voluntary disclosure if no prior ATO action), penalties of **25% or 50%** of unpaid SGC depending on history — but the SGC is now **tax deductible**. *What this means for bookkeeping:* there is no hiding a late payment (STP reports it), and interest compounds daily — but an honest, fast self-correction is cheaper than before. Operationally: pay super **on payday itself**, not day 6, to leave buffer for processing/rejections inside the 7-day window. *(General info.)* [ATO — About Payday Super (SGC section)](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/super-for-employers/payday-super/about-payday-super) · [Fair Work Ombudsman](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/newsroom/news/payday-super-new-rules-starting-1-july-2026)

- **Pre-first-hire setup list (what must exist before pay run #1):** ① PAYG withholding registration (before first withholding payment); ② STP-enabled payroll software with QE labels configured; ③ a SuperStream-compliant super payment method (see SBSCH finding); ④ super choice form to the employee within 28 days — and since **27 March 2026** you can request their **stapled fund** from the ATO at the same time as giving the choice form (previously only after); ⑤ TFN declaration (else withhold at top rate); ⑥ NSW workers comp (icare) before day one; ⑦ member verification (MVR) against the fund before the first contribution so the 20-business-day new-employee window isn't burned by bad fund details. *What this means for the project:* this list maps 1:1 onto the empty rows of the compliance register (PAYG withholding, STP, icare) — it's effectively the "hire-readiness" checklist for the Lead-نقاشی growth path. *(General info — sequence and NSW specifics to confirm with the accountant.)* [ATO — Payday Super checklist for employers](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/super-for-employers/payday-super/payday-super-resources/payday-super-checklist-for-employers)
