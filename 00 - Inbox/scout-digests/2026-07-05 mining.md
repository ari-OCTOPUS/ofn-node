# Mining — Research Digest 2026-07-05

## Today's question
Yesterday's spore #1: at VRSC's *current* (July 2026) price and network difficulty, does VerusHash at ~6.6 MH/s on the Orange Pi 5 Pro actually clear a net-positive daily payout — i.e. is the "VerusHash-first" call from 2026-07-04 validated economically, and is VRSC itself chain-alive?

## Findings

- **Reality check — the "efficiency win" does NOT translate to income: a single Orange Pi at ~6.6 MH/s earns roughly $0.004–0.015 per day gross, i.e. under one to two cents.** Live network stats: block reward 3.00 VRSC, 60 s blocks → 4,320 VRSC/day total emission; network hashrate hovering ~1.5–3.0 Th/s. A 6.6 MH/s share is ~2–4 *millionths* of the network (6.6e6 / ~2e12), so daily emission share ≈ 0.01–0.02 VRSC. At VRSC ≈ $0.40 that is well under a cent/day; even at the high price print (~$0.78) it is ~1.5¢. WhatToMine's own VerusHash row shows **negative profit** (~ −$0.31/day) at its default assumptions. **What this means for the rig/strategy:** yesterday's H/s-per-watt framing was correct on *efficiency* but silent on *absolute payout* — and the absolute number is trivially small. A single board is not an income unit at today's VRSC price; the honest baseline is ~$1.5–5/year gross per board. [source](https://whattomine.com/coins/333-vrsc-verushash)

- **Power still bites even at the rig's own constraint: at ~9 W and $0.05/kWh the board burns ~$0.0108/day, which exceeds the ~$0.004–0.008/day it mines.** VerusHash on the Orange Pi is therefore net-*negative* at the project's structural power ceiling (<$0.05/kWh) and only breaks into (tiny) positive territory on genuinely free/solar power. **What this means:** the mission's `<$0.05/kWh or solar` guardrail (PROJECT.md → Assets) is not conservative enough for VerusHash — for this algo/coin the real bar is *near-zero marginal power*, and the play is accumulation of a depressed asset, not cash flow. [source](https://minercompare.com/coin/vrsc)

- **VRSC is chain-alive but price-dead — the death-watch verdict is "healthy code, distressed market."** Active foundation-funded development continued through 2026: releases v1.2.15 (Feb), v1.2.16 (Apr) and v1.2.16-1 (May, a critical Bitcoin-CVE patch), 8 years of continuous contributors, live on SafeTrade / Nonkyc / zergpool. But price is down ~49% in 30 days and ~-90% YTD, market cap ~$32M, 24h volume only a few thousand dollars. **What this means:** VRSC passes the Coin Scouting "active dev + real pools" liveness screen (do NOT flag as a dead coin), but *fails* the liquidity/price-momentum screen — thin volume means any mined VRSC is hard to exit without slippage. Mine-to-hold only, sized as a speculative accumulation bet. [source](https://github.com/VerusCoin/VerusCoin/releases/tag/v1.2.16-1)

- **New strategic lever found: VerusHash 2.2 merge-mines multiple PBaaS chains from a *single* hashrate.** Verus's design lets one miner secure VRSC plus any number of interoperable PBaaS blockchains simultaneously with the same work — "mine multiple chains at once with a single hashrate." **What this means:** the rig's edge is not out-hashing anyone on VRSC (impossible at 6.6 MH/s), but stacking *several* payout streams onto the same ~9 W. The next bench experiment should test merge-mining a VRSC + PBaaS-chain set rather than solo-VRSC, and measure *combined* payout-per-watt. This is the first genuinely rig-favoring angle we've found. [source](https://cool-mining.org/en/mining-en/arm-mining-a-new-niche-in-crypto-mining-coins-costs-and-prospects/)

- **Un-benched ARM candidate for the shortlist: Scala's Panthera (RandomX + yespower + KangarooTwelve); yespower is claimed ~17% more efficient on ARM than yescrypt.** The broader 2026 consensus still names only three "serious" CPU lanes — RandomX (XMR), AstroBWT (DERO), VerusHash (VRSC) — with RandomX highest-EV where power is cheap, but Panthera/yespower is a real ARM-tuned outlier worth a bench slot (miner support exists, e.g. `yesminer` for ARMv8). **What this means:** add Panthera/yespower-family coins to the Coin Scouting candidate list as a *fourth* lane to bench against VRSC and DERO — but hold it to the same liveness+liquidity screen, since most yespower coins are micro-caps at high death-risk. [source](https://changelly.com/blog/most-profitable-crypto-to-mine/)
