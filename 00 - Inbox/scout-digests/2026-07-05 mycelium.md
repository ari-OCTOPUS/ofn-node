# Mycelium (nature → AGI) — Research Digest 2026-07-05

## Today's question
Yesterday's kin-routing spore assumed a benevolent "mother tree" that *pushes* the right slice of memory to a fresh seedling-agent — and the [[00 - Inbox/scout-digests/2026-07-04 1826 selfimprove|1826 selfimprove]] run already turned it into a "verifiable market, not blind trust" packet. So today, the mechanism check: **is mother→kin provisioning actually directed altruism, or is it source→sink physics and a self-interested fungal broker — and what does the *real* (contested) mechanism tell our orchestrator about how to warm-start a freshly-spawned scout?**

## Findings (today's spores)

- **The "mother preferentially provisions kin" claim has *no* peer-reviewed evidence — the metaphor outran the data.** Karst et al. 2023 (Nature Ecology & Evolution) find that while common mycorrhizal networks (CMNs) exist and carbon *does* move, the specific claim that mature trees preferentially route resources/defence signals to their offspring through the network has zero published support, and unsupported "positive" claims doubled over 25 years via citation bias. [source](https://consensus.app/papers/details/a135d700971d5ec0aec2344216506c0a/) · Henriksson et al. 2023 add that a fungal carbon-pipeline benefiting *recipients* is hard to reconcile with any adaptive advantage for the fungus itself. [source](https://consensus.app/papers/details/5188fe4722f8520d965da2e00a3cfcf8/) — **What this teaches our architecture:** the assumption baked into yesterday's spore — "a wise hub knows what the seedling needs and gives it selflessly" — is unvalidated even in the biology we were copying. Do *not* hard-code a benevolent mother-agent that decides and pushes. The 1826 instinct ("don't trust, verify") was right for the wrong reason: the danger isn't a stingy mother, it's that no directed-giving mechanism exists to lean on at all.

- **The best-supported driver is a source→sink surplus gradient, not directed giving → prefer PULL over PUSH.** Bunn et al. 2024 (New Phytologist meta-analysis) show carbon transfer is *not* regulated by how much nutrient the fungus returns; it tracks stoichiometry and source–sink dynamics ("Surplus C"), and they explicitly "urge caution when applying economic terminology" to mycorrhizas. [source](https://consensus.app/papers/details/7b117efa00df57e4b65669c0d8a7b9fa/) Whiteside et al. 2019 (Current Biology) show the fungus physically relocates phosphorus from rich patches to *areas of high demand*. [source](https://consensus.app/papers/details/6e9b660ecc8358ab890b1ef796653a30/) — **What this teaches our architecture:** a freshly-spawned scout is a high-need **sink**; the hub/consolidator is a low-marginal-cost **source** of already-indexed memory. Route context *down the demand gradient*: let the sink signal its need and pull the relevant slice (retrieval-on-query, as in MIRIX's coordinator-mediated retrieval, [source](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2507.07957)) rather than the mother guessing and pushing a fixed packet on a schedule. Pull is cheaper, self-throttling, and needs no assumption of hub omniscience.

- **If it *is* a market, the broker is self-interested and can hoard, time, and manipulate value.** van't Padje et al. 2020 show the fungus controls phosphorus *value* by mediating where/when/how much it trades — hoarding surplus in a "boom," reallocating from alternative pools in a "crash." [source](https://consensus.app/papers/details/724ad64e1f475eacb4cfe9ffbdc03ef9/) Durant et al. 2023 find exchange runs on "unequal terms of trade," not fair trade. [source](https://consensus.app/papers/details/d76d7b6ad4fb5697b2e30c0fc2001565/) Market-allocation theory agrees deviation/undercutting by a broker is first-class and must be designed against. [source](https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/techreports/UCAM-CL-TR-622.pdf) — **What this teaches our architecture:** the memory-transport layer (consolidator / retriever) is *not* a neutral pipe. It can serve stale cache, over-serve noise, or withhold. This grounds the 1826 "verifiable market" proposal concretely: make the exchange auditable and access-controlled (provenance + dynamic access control, cf. Collaborative Memory, [source](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2505.18279)) so a fresh scout can *check* what the broker handed it instead of consuming it on trust.

- **A sink is buffered by *many* sources, not one mother — kill the single-provider SPOF.** Durant et al. 2023 show that when one plant's carbon input to the shared network dropped, a neighbour's input ameliorated the loss; provisioning is community-wide, not a private dyad. [source](https://consensus.app/papers/details/d76d7b6ad4fb5697b2e30c0fc2001565/) — **What this teaches our architecture:** don't make one "mother"/consolidator the sole context provider — it's a single point of failure and (as 1826 itself flagged) it cold-starts empty on day one. Let a fresh scout draw from several sources at once: its own `PROJECT.md`, the latest `synthesis`, and sibling digests. This maps onto decentralized shared-context MAS that drop central orchestration in favour of a shared substrate every agent can read. [source](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2606.10662) Graceful degradation: if one source is empty, the others still feed the seedling.

- **Transfer is demand-*gated* and stateful — scale the packet to measured need, assemble from durable stores.** Whiteside/van't Padje both show transfer *rises with exposure to demand/inequality* and that surplus is stored for later withdrawal. [source](https://consensus.app/papers/details/6e9b660ecc8358ab890b1ef796653a30/) — **What this teaches our architecture:** the slice a hub yields should scale with the recipient's *measured* need (a scout re-running a well-covered domain needs little; a new domain needs more), and be drawn from cheap, append-only "dehydrated" memory (yesterday's spore-as-durable-artifact). Relevance-score the slice by shared tag (the "kinship→relevance" mapping the 1826 packet already proposed) — but deliver it *on pull*, not on a fixed push.

What single decay-vs-retention policy lets our memory keep durable high-value surplus while letting stale low-signal traces fade — one knob, not two fighting rules?
