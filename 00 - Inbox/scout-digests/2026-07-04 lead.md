# Lead (painting, Sydney) — Research Digest 2026-07-04 (live kickoff)

## Today's question
For a Sydney house-painting business starting from zero, which lead channel gives the cheapest qualified leads per dollar in 2025–2026 — pay-per-lead marketplaces (hipages/Oneflare/Airtasker), organic local SEO / Google Business Profile, or paid ads — and where does AI-assisted lead farming actually fit?

## Findings
- **Marketplace leads are pay-to-compete: hipages runs ~$200–$600/mo subscription PLUS $30–$80+ per accepted lead, and Oneflare uses $1 = 1 credit quoting; every lead is sold to multiple tradies at once.** This means marketplace CPL only looks cheap until you factor the win-rate against 3–5 rivals quoting the same job — the real cost per *won* job is a multiple of the sticker CPL. Best used to fill gaps, not as the primary channel. [source](https://www.20minutemarketing.com.au/blog/hipages-alternative)
- **Ranking in Google's top-3 Local Pack yields ~45–78 qualified leads/month vs 8–15/month for tradies stuck beyond page one, and ~42% of local searchers click a Map Pack result.** For a Sydney painter, a well-optimised Google Business Profile (per-service listings like "Interior Painting"/"Exterior Painting", NAP consistency, up to 20 suburb service areas) is the highest-leverage owned channel — leads are effectively free after the SEO work, unlike marketplace CPL. [source](https://growthlocal.com.au/post/local-seo-for-tradies-in-australia)
- **Google Local Services Ads (the "Google Guaranteed" / now "Google Verified" pay-per-lead product) are NOT available in Australia — only US/Canada/UK + a handful of EU countries.** Any plan that assumed LSAs for Sydney is dead on arrival; the closest local pay-per-lead options remain hipages, Oneflare, Airtasker and ServiceSeeking. Note also the badge consolidated from "Google Guaranteed"/"Google Screened" into a single "Google Verified" blue check on 20 Oct 2025. [source](https://searchscope.com.au/local-seo/google-local-service-ads-for-businesses/)
- **Airtasker charges no browse fee but takes a ~15%+ service fee on job value and is strongest at the small-job / one-room end of the market.** Different economics from hipages/Oneflare (percentage-of-job vs per-lead), so it suits low-ticket touch-up work and cash-flow bootstrapping rather than full-house repaints — useful as a portfolio/review builder early on. [source](https://tradiecard.au/resources/is-airtasker-worth-it-for-tradies-australia)
- **Paid-search painting keywords run roughly $15–$75 per lead, and one Google Ads lead reportedly converts like 3–4 Facebook leads; "digital door-knocking" (targeting homeowners near active jobsites) is flagged as the most cost-effective residential tactic for 2025.** Google Ads is a controllable, scalable channel where owned SEO is still ramping, and geo-targeting around current projects concentrates spend on warm neighbourhoods. [source](https://www.glasshouse.biz/blog/lead-generation-painting-residential)
- **AI-assisted lead farming works best delivered as a managed service: the vendor handles prompt engineering / API integration so the tradie only sees captured, scored, auto-followed-up leads — adoption hinges on owner digital self-efficacy, trust and vendor support.** For our Lead-نقاشی play this validates positioning AI as the invisible back-end (lead capture, scoring, 24/7 chatbot booking, quote/follow-up automation saving ~10 hrs/week) rather than asking the painter to operate tools. [source](https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202512.0295)
