# NCCIH: Hypnosis overview

Hypnosis is focused attention with guided relaxation or imagery. Evidence suggests it may help some painful conditions and selected clinical contexts. It should not replace standard medical care and self-hypnosis should be practiced in a safe place, not during activities requiring alertness.
