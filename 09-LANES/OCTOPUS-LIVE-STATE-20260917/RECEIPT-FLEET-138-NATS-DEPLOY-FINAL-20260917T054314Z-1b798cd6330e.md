## Residual / honest scope note

The leaf currently has **no persistent consumer** on the hub — `"streams": 0` and no long-lived
subscriber for `octopus.telemetry.*`. The connection and the publish path are proven, but telemetry
emitted between now and whenever a consumer or JetStream stream exists will be dropped by design
(NATS core has no persistence). If the fleet is meant to accumulate telemetry, the hub needs a
stream or a durable consumer; otherwise this is a live link with nothing listening.
