# CH-07 · Health Score Composite

> **Channel type:** Observability / composite health metric
> **Status:** ✅ Implemented
> **Wave:** 2 (P1 channel)

---

## Purpose

A single 0–100 score that answers: *"How healthy is the organism right now?"*

Derived from three orthogonal axes, weighted and normalized.

---

## Files

| Role | Path | LOC |
|---|---|---|
| **Extractor** | `nervous-system/extract_health_score.py` | ~341 |
| **Data output** | `nervous-system/health-data.js` | auto |
| **Weights config** | `nervous-system/health-weights.json` | optional |

---

## Formula

```
Health = 0.40 × system_health
       + 0.35 × fitness_health
       + 0.25 × telemetry_health
       − min(10, alert_count × 2)
```

Result clamped to `[0, 100]`.

---

## Sub-scores

### 1. System Health (40%)
**Source:** `ORGANISM-STATE.json`

| Factor | Penalty | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Wires off | −3 per wire (max −30) | `wiring` flags |
| Frozen | −40 | `frozen == true` |
| Halted | −30 | `halted == true` |
| Germline lag | −0.5 per hour (max −15) | `germline_lag_h` |
| Effects pending | −5 | `effects_pending > 10` |
| Cardiac depleted | −10 | `cardiac.budget.depleted` |
| Cartographer stale | −5 | `cartographer.map_stale` |

### 2. Fitness Health (35%)
**Source:** `fitness-latest.json`

| Factor | Penalty | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Low cell fitness | proportional | `avg_fit × 100` |
| Integrity alerts | −15 each (max −30) | `integrity_alerts` count |
| Not authoritative | −5 | `authoritative == false` |

Neutral baseline = 50 if no cells yet.

### 3. Telemetry Health (25%)
**Source:** `telemetry-latest.json` + `ORGANISM-STATE.json`

| Factor | Penalty | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Suspect zeros | −3 each (max −15) | `suspect_zero_total` |
| Spend > 80% | −10 | monthly AUD cap |
| Spend > 95% | −20 | monthly AUD cap |
| Conflicts | −15 | `conflicts.length > 0` |
| Unmapped spend | −5 | `unmapped_musd` non-empty |

---

## Alert Penalty

Active governor alerts (last 24h from `governor-alerts.md`):
- Each alert: −2 (max −10)
