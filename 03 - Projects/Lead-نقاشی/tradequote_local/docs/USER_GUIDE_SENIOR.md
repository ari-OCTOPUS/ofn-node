# TradeQuote Local — Your Guide

Everything lives on your phone. No internet needed, no account, no fees.
Every screen has one big blue button — that is always the next step.

## First time only: set up your business

1. Open the app. 2. Type your business name. 3. Type your ABN if you have
it handy (you can add it later). 4. Turn on "Registered for GST" if you
charge GST. 5. Add your bank details so they appear on invoices.
6. Press **Save and start**. You can change all of this later in Settings.

## Make a quote

1. Press **NEW QUOTE**. 2. Tap the customer, or **Add new customer** and
just type their name. 3. Say what work you will do — the grey buttons fill
in common jobs for you. 4. Press **Next** and type ONE price for the job
(the app works out the GST). 5. Press **Next**, check the total, press
**SEND QUOTE**.

If you press Back or answer a call, nothing is lost — the quote waits for
you on the Home screen under **Continue where you left off**.

## Send it

- **SEND WITH TELEGRAM** — your phone's share menu opens; tap Telegram,
  pick the person, send.
- **PREPARE IN GMAIL** — Gmail opens with the email written and the PDF
  attached; you just press Send. (If you leave Gmail without sending,
  Gmail keeps it in its own Drafts.)
- **More sharing options** — WhatsApp, SMS, anything else on your phone.
- **Save PDF to phone** — keeps a copy in your files.

When you come back, the app asks **"What happened?"** Tap **I sent it** and
the quote is marked as sent. The app never guesses — only you can say a
document was sent.

## When the customer says yes

Open the quote and press **CUSTOMER SAID YES**, then **MAKE INVOICE**.
The invoice gets its own number and the quote stays in your records
untouched.

## When they pay you

Open the invoice, press **RECORD PAYMENT**. The full amount is already
filled in — change it if they paid part. The invoice shows **Paid**,
**Partly paid**, or **OVERDUE** in plain words. To nudge a slow payer,
press **Send reminder** — a polite message with the invoice attached.

## Keep your data safe

Settings → **My data** → **BACK UP MY DATA**. One file holds everything.
Save it to your phone, or tap Google Drive in the list if you have it
(Drive needs internet just for that moment). To move to a new phone:
install the app, Settings → My data → **Restore from a backup file**.
Your current data is copied safely first, so restore can't destroy
anything.

## Good to know

- Sending opens Telegram or Gmail for you — it cannot confirm the message
  arrived. If unsure, check Telegram/Gmail like normal.
- Changing your business details later never changes invoices you already
  sent — those are locked, as the tax office expects.
- Text too small? Settings → **Display** → Extra large.
- This app keeps your records. For tax lodgment and advice, your
  accountant is still the boss.
