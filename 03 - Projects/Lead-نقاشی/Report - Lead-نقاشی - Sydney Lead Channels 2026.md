## 3. Ranked channel mix — solo painter, small budget

1. **Google Business Profile + review engine (start week 1, never stop)** — free, exclusive leads, compounding; primary category + weekly review velocity are the levers.
2. **One marketplace as volume floor** — ServiceSeeking Starter ($89/3mo, no commission) as cheap test, or hipages if suburb volume justifies it; strict rules: only accept leads matching profitable job profile, respond within minutes (lead is shared 2–3 ways).
3. **Airtasker for gap-filling** — zero upfront cost, commission only on wins; use Bronze→Gold tier progression to cut fees from 20%→~12.5%.
4. **Letterbox radius drops around every active job** — cheapest offline lead source; 500–1,000 flyers per job street.
5. **Strata/PM track (start month 2-3)** — compliance pack first (licence #, insurance certificate, SWMS template), then approach 5–10 Sydney strata managers; this is the escape route from marketplace bidding wars.
6. **Meta lead ads (only after 1–5 running)** — $500–1,000/mo test with instant follow-up; else leads rot.
7. **Google Ads last** — at ~$138 CPL / $13.74 CPC, only viable with tight geo-targeting, a converting landing page, and quote follow-up discipline.

## 4. 90-day action plan

**Days 1–14 (foundation):** Claim/complete GBP (primary category "Painter", service areas, photos of real jobs); ask last 10 happy customers for reviews; set up review-request SMS template; verify NSW licence displayed everywhere; join ServiceSeeking Starter; create Airtasker profile.
**Days 15–30 (volume test):** Respond to every marketplace lead <15 min; log every lead (source, cost, quoted, won) in a simple sheet — this log is the whole strategy later; first letterbox drop 1,000 flyers around current job; 2 reviews/week cadence.
**Days 31–60 (quality shift):** Build strata compliance pack (insurance cert, SWMS, product-spec quote template naming coating system/coats/DFT — what strata managers require); email/call 10 strata & property managers; ask 3 builders/REAs for subcontract or referral arrangement; kill any marketplace whose cost-per-won-job exceeds ~10% of job value.
**Days 61–90 (scale what works):** Read the lead log — double the best channel, cut the worst; if GBP enquiries are flowing, start a $30–50/day Meta lead-ads test with missed-call text-back in place; review velocity check (target: rank movement in Maps for "painter <suburb>"); decide whether hipages/Oneflare deserve any budget at all based on YOUR numbers, not theirs.
