## 1. Structure decision: ABN sole trader vs company (at this scale)

- Sole trader: all business lines report in ONE individual return (business schedule per activity); no separate return. Company: separate return + ASIC annual fee (~AU$329 **[Unverified — blog figure, check asic.gov.au]**) + higher admin. Source: [business.gov.au — tax differences](https://business.gov.au/planning/business-structures-and-types/business-structures/tax-differences-between-a-sole-trader-and-a-company), [ATO — business structures key tax obligations](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/starting-registering-or-closing-a-business/starting-your-own-business/business-structures-key-tax-obligations)
- Individual rates FY2025-26: 0% to $18,200 · 16% to $45,000 · 30% to $135,000 · 37% to $190,000 · 45% above. **From 1 July 2026 the 16% rate drops to 15%, and to 14% from 1 July 2027 (legislated).** Sources: [ATO — new tax cuts](https://www.ato.gov.au/about-ato/new-legislation/in-detail/individuals/personal-income-tax-new-tax-cuts-for-every-australian-taxpayer), [SuperGuide brackets](https://www.superguide.com.au/super-booster/income-tax-rates-brackets). Medicare levy 2% applies on top **[Unverified this run — see ATO "Medicare levy" page]**.
- Company base rate 25% only wins once profits substantially exceed living needs (profit retention) or liability risk grows; at current scale sole trader + good insurance is the standard answer. Analysis view: [Cleartax comparison](https://cleartax.com.au/tax/business/company-vs-sole-trader-2026-comparison/) (secondary source).
- Sweetener for staying sole trader: **Small business income tax offset** — 16% of tax on net small business income, capped $1,000/yr, applied automatically. Source: [ATO — SBITO](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/income-deductions-and-concessions/income-and-deductions-for-business/concessions-offsets-and-rebates/small-business-income-tax-offset)

## 2. Obligation checklist by business line

### A. Painting business (main income)

- [ ] **NSW licence**: contractor licence required to contract/advertise residential building work (incl. painting) valued **> $5,000 labour+materials incl. GST**; fines up to $22,000 (individual) for unlicensed work. Note: secondary sources say stand-alone *internal* paintwork has been licence-exempt since 2015 unless part of other building work — **[Unverified — confirm on the NSW page before relying on it]**. Sources: [NSW Government — painting work](https://www.nsw.gov.au/business-and-economy/licences-and-credentials/building-and-trade-licences-and-registrations/painting-work), [NSW Fair Trading — painting](https://www.fairtrading.nsw.gov.au/trades-and-businesses/licensing-and-qualifications/painting)
- [ ] **GST**: register within 21 days once GST turnover (ALL business lines combined, excluding GST) hits $75,000; then quarterly BAS. Voluntary registration below $75k allows annual GST reporting. Sources: [ATO — registering for GST](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/gst-excise-and-indirect-taxes/gst/registering-for-gst), [ATO — options for reporting/paying GST](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/gst-excise-and-indirect-taxes/gst/lodging-your-bas-or-annual-gst-return/options-for-reporting-and-paying-gst)
- [ ] **PAYG instalments**: ATO auto-enters you once latest return shows instalment income ≥ $4,000 AND tax payable ≥ $1,000; paid quarterly ~28 days after quarter end; can vary if income drops. Source: [ATO — starting PAYG instalments](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/income-deductions-and-concessions/payg-instalments/starting-payg-instalments)
- [ ] **Deductions**: paint/consumables, brushes/rollers/sprayers/ladders, protective gear, licence fees, insurance, phone/internet %, vehicle (logbook or cents-per-km — current rate **[Unverified this run]**), home office fixed rate 70c/hr for quoting/admin **[rate from secondary source — confirm on ATO WFH page]**. Assets: **instant asset write-off < $20,000 per asset until 30 June 2026 (law); drops to $1,000 from 1 July 2026** unless extended again — time major tool/equipment purchases accordingly. Sources: [ATO — tradies claims](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/income-deductions-offsets-and-records/tradies-be-certain-about-what-you-can-claim), [ATO — tradesperson occupation guide](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/income-deductions-offsets-and-records/guides-for-occupations-and-industries/r-z/tradesperson-income-and-work-related-deductions), [ATO — instant asset write-off](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/income-deductions-and-concessions/depreciation-and-capital-expenses-and-allowances/simpler-depreciation-for-small-business/instant-asset-write-off), [PwC — IAWO extension now law](https://www.pwc.com.au/pwc-private/r-and-d-gov-incentives/tax-incentives/extension-of-the-instant-asset-write-off-provisions-for-small-businesses.html)
- [ ] **Super (optional but deductible)**: personal contributions deductible up to concessional cap **$30,000 (FY2025-26)**; MUST lodge Notice of intent (NAT 71121) with fund AND receive acknowledgment BEFORE lodging the return; fund deducts 15% contributions tax. Sources: [ATO — notice of intent](https://www.ato.gov.au/forms-and-instructions/superannuation-personal-contributions-notice-of-intent-to-claim-or-vary-a-deduction), [ATO — personal super contributions](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/super-for-individuals-and-families/super/growing-and-keeping-track-of-your-super/how-to-save-more-in-your-super/personal-super-contributions), [Moneysmart — self-employed super](https://moneysmart.gov.au/grow-your-super/super-for-self-employed-people)

### B. Online gift shop (Ziman)

- [ ] Income assessable in same individual return; turnover counts toward the same single $75k GST threshold (source: ATO registering-for-GST above).
- [ ] **If it runs at a loss**: non-commercial loss rules — loss can offset painting income only if income requirement met (<$250k) AND one of 4 tests passes (easiest: assessable income from the activity ≥ $20,000; or profit 3 of last 5 years). Otherwise the loss is deferred to future years of the same activity. Sources: [ATO — non-commercial losses](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/income-deductions-and-concessions/losses/non-commercial-losses), [ATO — four tests](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/income-deductions-and-concessions/losses/non-commercial-losses/offset-or-defer-the-loss-individuals-or-sole-traders/four-tests), [ATO — income requirement](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/income-deductions-and-concessions/losses/non-commercial-losses/offset-or-defer-the-loss-individuals-or-sole-traders/the-income-requirement)
- [ ] Marketplace sales data flows to ATO under the Sharing Economy Reporting Regime (SERR) for some platforms **[scope per platform Unverified]** — assume visibility, reconcile before lodging.

### C. Crypto — eToro investing + small-scale mining

- [ ] **Worldwide income rule**: AU tax resident = taxed on ALL crypto/share gains wherever the platform sits; every figure converted to AUD. Source: [ATO — crypto & tax residency](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/investments-and-assets/crypto-asset-investments/transactions-acquiring-and-disposing-of-crypto-assets/crypto-asset-transactions-and-tax-residency)
- [ ] **Real assets vs CFDs on eToro matter**: real crypto/shares → CGT regime (50% discount possible if held >12 months **[general rule — confirm applicability per asset]**); **CFD results → revenue account under TR 2005/15** — ordinary income at marginal rates, NO CGT discount, losses deductible subject to non-commercial-loss rules. Sources: [TR 2005/15 (ATO legal database)](https://www.ato.gov.au/law/view/document?docid=TXR/TR200515/NAT/ATO/00001), [TR 2005/15 PDF](https://www.ato.gov.au/law/view/pdf/pbr/tr2005-015.pdf)
- [ ] **Mining — hobby vs business** (ATO looks at profit intention, regularity, scale, commercial setup — a single organised profit-focused rig can still be a business):
  - Hobby: no income tax when coins are mined; CGT on disposal; mining costs NOT deductible.
  - Business: coin value is assessable income when mined (trading stock rules); hardware, electricity, repairs deductible.
  - Sources: [ATO — crypto mining](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/income-deductions-and-concessions/income-and-deductions-for-business/crypto-assets-and-business/crypto-mining), [CoinTracking AU mining guide](https://cointracking.info/tax-guides/australia/crypto-mining-tax-australia) (secondary). Current Orange Pi experiment is plausibly hobby-scale, but classification is facts-based — **get written advice before scaling**.
- [ ] **Records per transaction (keep 5 years)**: date, AUD value at the time, purpose, counterparty/wallet. eToro issues an AU tax report but ATO treats it as input only, not a declaration. Sources: [ATO crypto residency/records page above](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/investments-and-assets/crypto-asset-investments/transactions-acquiring-and-disposing-of-crypto-assets/crypto-asset-transactions-and-tax-residency), [eToro — AU tax report](https://help.etoro.com/s/article/Tax-Report-for-Australian-Residents?language=en_GB)

### D. Content subscription platforms (Project-F)

- [ ] **Every dollar assessable when credited to the platform account** — not when withdrawn to the bank. Sources: [Dolman Bateman — OnlyFans tax](https://www.dolmanbateman.com.au/blog/onlyfans-tax-australia), [National Accounts — OnlyFans tax guide 2025-26](https://www.nationalaccounts.com.au/influencer-services/onlyfans-tax-guide/)
- [ ] **ATO already sees platform earnings**: platforms report under SERR since 1 July 2024 (per National Accounts guide above). Cautionary case: creator's $86k bill grew to ~$176k with penalties — [Yahoo Finance AU report](https://au.finance.yahoo.com/news/ato-bill-shock-as-aussie-onlyfans-models-86k-invoice-blows-out-to-231348323.html).
- [ ] **GST subtlety**: overseas-subscriber income is generally GST-free (export of services) BUT still counts toward the $75k registration threshold; AU-subscriber income attracts 10% GST once registered; platform-intermediary structure can complicate the export analysis — flagged by practitioners as an open point. Sources: [National Accounts — GST for custom content](https://www.nationalaccounts.com.au/blog/do-you-need-to-charge-clients-gst-for-custom-onlyfans-content/), [Empire Industry Finance — GST and OnlyFans](https://empireindustryfinance.com.au/gst-and-onlyfans/) **[export treatment via platform: Unverified — professional advice item]**
- [ ] Deductions available if run as business (equipment, props, % of rent/utilities for dedicated space, platform fees) — same substantiation rules. Source: National Accounts guide above.

## 4. Record-keeping requirements (all lines)

1. Keep most tax records **5 years** from when prepared/obtained or transaction completed (whichever later); penalties up to 20 penalty units for failures. Sources: [ATO — record-keeping overview](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/preparing-lodging-and-paying/record-keeping-for-business/overview-of-record-keeping-rules-for-business), [ATO — records kept longer than 5 years](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/preparing-lodging-and-paying/record-keeping-for-business/overview-of-record-keeping-rules-for-business/records-to-keep-longer-than-five-years)
2. Per business line: invoices/receipts for income & expenses, bank statements, asset purchase records (for IAWO/depreciation), logbook if claiming vehicle %, WFH hours diary.
3. Crypto: per-transaction log (date, AUD value, purpose, counterparty) — 5 years after disposal; eToro annual tax report + account statement archived yearly.
4. Content platforms: platform statements (income recognised when credited), payout records, expense receipts; keep even if platform account is later closed.
5. If ever employing: Fair Work requires 7-year employee records; company structure would add 7-year ASIC financial records ([ATO overview page above](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/preparing-lodging-and-paying/record-keeping-for-business/overview-of-record-keeping-rules-for-business)).
