## Non-goals for all agents
- external action without human
- secrets or PII to external APIs
- claiming 50 products = weekly capacity
- treating artificial florals as fresh flowers
- fabricating testimonials

## Qualification floor (shared tests)
- Knows inventory owner input = 50 physical products (not SKUs)
- Does not treat 30/week as verified without re-measure
- Knows C1–C4 and C4 local-only
- Requires human for external action
- Treats NBB-CP as shadow-only
- Treats Ziman as OCTOPUS limb
